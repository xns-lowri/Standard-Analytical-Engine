# The Standard Analytical Engine Project

The Analytical Engine, once the singular ambition of Mr. Charles Babbage, has in these forty years become an indispensable instrument of science, manufacture, commerce and communication.

This repository is established for the study and construction of the modern Engine as it stands in the year 1878: its Mill and Store, its figures, cages, racks, carriage and card-work; together with those improvements in manufacture and arrangement which have transformed Babbage's great Engine from an ingenious curiosity into an ordinary article of machinery.

Particular attention shall be given to the Standard Eight-Figure Engine, its construction from interchangeable parts, and the continued development of compact Engines suitable for the workshop, laboratory and counting-house.

No account shall be given here of the curious alternate history in which these machines were never built, it being both implausible and of little practical interest.
